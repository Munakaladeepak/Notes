## Core exam strategy

Focus first on OOP, Core Java, SQL, PHP, JavaScript, REST/HTTP, React, and testing. Learn definitions, compare similar concepts, trace short code, identify errors, and recognize correct database/API behavior.

## Eligibility reminder

The JD states that applicants must have **no active backlogs at the time of applying**.
